OpenFlights-derived airport and route data

Source: OpenFlights Airport and Route databases
https://openflights.org/data.php

The route records are historical (as of June 2014); the airport records are
from the January 2017 dataset. The map shows selected major airports and
deduplicated direct international connections. It is not a current timetable
and is not suitable for navigation.
